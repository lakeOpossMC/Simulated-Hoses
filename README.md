Simulated Hoses adds physics-based hoses to Create: Simulated and Sable. Inspired by VS / Sable Hose Connectors by M_K2525.

BLOCKS AND ITEMS:
- Hose Coupler: Functions like a Rope Coupler that can transport fluids.
- Hose Connector: A fluid port. Only connects to Hoses.
- Magnetic Hose Connector: Magnetic variant of the Hose Connector. Powering two connectors with opposing poles causes them to attract eachother!
- Hose Winch: Fluid version of the Rope Winch. When a Magnetic Hose Connector is attached, powering the Winch powers the connector.
- Fluid Quick-Connect: A placeable fluid port. Carry a connector close and it locks on.
- Pressure Nozzle: Place on a Hose Connector, then carry it and hold attack to spray any fluid.

FLUIDS:
- Plant Food: A thick, nutritious green goo made by mixing Tree Fertilizer and Sugar with water.
